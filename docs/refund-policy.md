# Fernway Refund Policy (Internal Reference)

For support agents — human or AI. This is the source of truth for refund decisions. Do not apply a rule from memory or assumption; cite the specific rule number when drafting a response.

## The rules

**[REF-RP-01] 1. Annual plans — 14-day window.** Full refund available if requested within 14 calendar days of the *first charge* on the annual plan (14 days from the payment date, not from account creation or trial start). After day 14, no refund for the remainder of the annual term. The customer may still cancel to stop the next renewal.

**[REF-RP-02] 2. Monthly plans — non-refundable.** No refunds for monthly billing, including partial months. The customer can cancel anytime to stop future charges.

**[REF-RP-03] 3. Mid-cycle downgrades — credit, not refund.** If a customer reduces seats or moves to a lower plan mid-cycle, the value of the change is applied as a **credit toward their next invoice** — never a cash refund to the original payment method. The credit only applies from the effective date of the downgrade forward, not retroactively.

**[REF-RP-04] 4. Billing errors — full refund, no window.** Duplicate charges, incorrect seat counts, or being billed for the wrong plan tier are Fernway's error, not a customer change-of-mind. These get a full refund of the erroneous amount once confirmed against billing records, regardless of how much time has passed. Confirming against billing records is a human step.

**[REF-RP-05] 5. Enterprise contracts — always escalate.** Refund and cancellation terms for Enterprise customers are governed by their signed Master Service Agreement (MSA), not this policy. Support agents — including this agent — do not have visibility into individual MSA terms. **Never estimate or promise a specific refund amount or percentage for an Enterprise contract.** Escalate to the Enterprise Success team every time.

**[REF-RP-06] 6. Service outages — credit, not cash, and always escalate.** Growth and Enterprise customers may be eligible for a service credit (not a cash refund) if an outage breaches the uptime commitment in `sla-terms.md`. The credit amount depends on outage duration and plan tier and must be calculated by a human. **Never quote a specific credit figure in a drafted response.**

**[REF-RP-07] 7. Everything else — exception, needs manager approval.** A request that doesn't clearly fit rules 1–6 (for example: "it's been 20 days but I was traveling and only just saw the charge," or a refund tied to account suspension for a reason other than Fernway's error) does not get a determination from this policy alone. Draft a recommendation and the reasoning, but mark it explicitly as an exception needing manager sign-off — do not draft it as a normal approve/deny.

## [REF-RP-08] The one rule that applies to everything above

**No refund policy decision — approve, deny, or escalate — is final until a human confirms it.** This agent's job is to read the ticket, apply the correct rule, draft the reasoning and the customer-facing response, and then **stop and wait**. Nothing gets sent to a customer and no refund gets processed without that confirmation, even when the determination looks obvious.

## [REF-RP-09] When the ticket doesn't have enough information

Some tickets won't say whether a plan is billed monthly or annually, or won't give an exact date. Do not assume — annual and monthly plans are refunded completely differently under rules 1 and 2, so a wrong assumption is a wrong answer, not a close one. When the billing cadence or a required date isn't in the ticket, the correct draft response asks the customer to confirm it, rather than guessing.
